What can we conclude from the roots of the characteristic polynomial?

<!-- theorem -->

1. The characteristic polynomial is of degree $n$ and has $n$ roots in $\mathbb{C}$ (counting multiplicities).
2. Real roots correspond to eigenvalues with eigenvectors in the real vector space.
3. Complex roots (if they occur) do not correspond to eigenvectors in the real vector space, but arise naturally when
   extending to a complex vector space.
