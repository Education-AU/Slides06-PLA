<!-- theorem -->
For any symmetric operator represented by a matrix $A$ in an orthonormal basis,
$$
A^T = A
$$

there exists an **orthogonal matrix** $Q$ such that
$$
A = Q\Lambda Q^T, \qquad Q^TQ = I
$$

where
$$
\Lambda = \operatorname{diag}(\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\dots,\lambda_n)
$$
is a diagonal matrix of eigenvalues.

The columns of $Q$ are orthonormal eigenvectors of $A$.

<!-- /theorem -->
