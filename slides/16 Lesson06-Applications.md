---
title: Eigenspace Decomposition
template: theorem
theoremTitle: Direct sum of eigenspaces (symmetric case)

---

<!-- theorem -->

Let $L: V \to V$ be a symmetric operator on a finite-dimensional vector space $V$. Then

$$
V = \bigoplus_{\lambda} E_\lambda,
$$

where $E_\lambda$ denotes the eigenspace corresponding to $\lambda$.

Moreover, for symmetric operators, each eigenvalue satisfies:

$$
\text{algebraic multiplicity} = \dim (E_\lambda).
$$
