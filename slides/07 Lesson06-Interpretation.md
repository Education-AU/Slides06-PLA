<div class="center-left">

An eigenvector is a direction $\mathbf{v}$ along which the operator acts by simple scaling:

$$
L\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}
$$

In this direction, the vector is only scaled (possibly reversed or collapsed).

A symmetric operator has a complete set of such directions, forming an orthonormal basis.

Vectors that are not eigenvectors are generally transformed by scaling in different directions.
</div>
