Let $L: V \to V$ be a **symmetric** linear operator on a real vector space $V$.

<!-- theorem -->

Let $\lambda$ be an eigenvalue of a linear operator $L$.

The **eigenspace** corresponding to $\lambda$ is
$$
E_\lambda = \ker (L - \lambda I)
$$

1. Since kernels are subspaces, $E_\lambda$ is a subspace of $V$
2. All nonzero vectors in $E_\lambda$ are eigenvectors corresponding to $\lambda$
3. The zero vector is included for algebraic completeness, but it is not an eigenvector.

<!-- /theorem -->
