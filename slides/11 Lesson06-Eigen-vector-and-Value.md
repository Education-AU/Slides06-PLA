theoremTitle: Eigenvector and Eigen value

Let $L: V \to V$ be a linear operator on a real vector space $V$.

<!-- theorem -->

A **non-zero** vector $\mathbf{v} \in V$ is called an **eigenvector** of $L$ if there exists a
scalar $\lambda \in \mathbb{R}$ such that
$$
L\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v}.
$$

The scalar $\lambda$ is called the **eigenvalue associated with $\mathbf{v}$**.

<!-- /theorem -->
