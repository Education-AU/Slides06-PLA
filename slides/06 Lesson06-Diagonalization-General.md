<!-- theorem -->
Let $L$ be any operator $L:V\rightarrow V$ on a finite dimensional vector space represented by a matrix $A$.

If $A$ has $n$ linearly independent eigenvectors, then there exists an invertible matrix $E$ such that
$$
A = E \Lambda E^{-1},
$$
where
$$
\Lambda = \operatorname{diag} (\lambda_1,\lambda_2,\dots,\lambda_n).
$$

The columns of $E$ are eigenvectors of $A$, but in general they are **not orthogonal**.
